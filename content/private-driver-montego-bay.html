Why Visit

Your Port Day, Your Route

A private driver tour in Montego Bay gives cruise passengers something group excursions cannot — flexibility. You set the priorities: Rose Hall in the morning and Doctor's Cave Beach in the afternoon, or a shopping stop on the Hip Strip with a late lunch overlooking the Caribbean.

Licensed private drivers know Jamaica's north coast roads, traffic patterns and cruise port timing. They track your ship's schedule and build return-to-ship buffer into the day — essential when you are not tied to a fixed group itinerary.

How It Fits Into a Cruise Day

Private driver tours typically begin with pickup at the Montego Bay cruise port and follow a pre-agreed route. Realistic half-day combinations include Rose Hall plus Doctor's Cave Beach, or Rose Hall plus Hip Strip shopping. Dunn's River Falls is possible on long port days but consumes most of your time in transit.

Discuss your all aboard time upfront. A good driver will push back if your wish list is unrealistic — that honesty protects your return to ship confidence.

Who It Is Best For

Couples wanting privacy and pace control. Small groups (typically up to four passengers) who can split cost. Repeat visitors who have done Dunn's River and want a tailored Montego Bay experience. Less cost-effective for solo travellers compared to joining a group shore excursion.

Practical Information

Booking Tips

Book through established operators with cruise port experience. Confirm vehicle type, maximum passengers, price inclusions and cancellation policy before you dock.

Entry Fees

Private driver rates usually cover transport only. Attraction entry fees (Rose Hall, Doctor's Cave, caves) are typically paid separately — budget accordingly.

Private Driver FAQ

Can you hire a private driver in Montego Bay?›

Yes. Private driver tours are widely available for Montego Bay cruise passengers. Book ahead for busy port days and confirm pickup at the cruise terminal.

Do private drivers return to the ship on time?›

Reputable licensed drivers track ship schedules and build buffer time. Share your all aboard time at the start of the day and keep the itinerary realistic.

Can a private driver take you to Dunn's River Falls?›

Yes, on a long port day. The drive is 60–90 minutes each way — your driver should advise whether timing allows a falls visit plus other stops. Read our Dunn's River guide first.
