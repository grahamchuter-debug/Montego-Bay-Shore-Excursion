Why Visit

Montego Bay's Most Famous Beach

Doctor's Cave Beach has drawn visitors to Montego Bay since the 1920s, when its clear turquoise water was said to have healing properties. Today it remains the go-to beach for cruise passengers who want an easy, beautiful swim without a long drive from the Montego Bay cruise port.

The beach sits on the Hip Strip (Gloucester Avenue), surrounded by resorts, jerk stands and shops. Facilities include changing rooms, showers and beach chair rentals — practical details that matter when you have only a few hours ashore.

  • ✔ ~10 minutes from the cruise port by taxi
  • ✔ Calm, clear water ideal for swimming
  • ✔ Entry fee applies — bring cash

How It Fits Into a Cruise Day

A Doctor's Cave Beach shore excursion typically includes round-trip transport from the Montego Bay cruise port, 2–3 hours at the beach, and return with plenty of buffer before all aboard. Many passengers combine the beach with a walk along the Hip Strip for lunch or souvenir shopping.

This is the most schedule-flexible Montego Bay shore excursion — ideal when your ship arrives late, departs early, or when you simply want a low-stress Jamaica port day.

Who It Is Best For

Families with children who want calm water and facilities. Couples seeking a romantic beach morning. First-time visitors who want a classic Montego Bay experience without long drives. Short port calls where adventure tours to Dunn's River would be too rushed.

Practical Information

Entry & Facilities

Doctor's Cave Beach charges an entry fee (cash helpful). Changing rooms, showers and chair rentals are available on site.

Getting There

Organised beach excursions include transport. Independent visitors can take a licensed taxi from the cruise port — agree on price and return pickup before leaving.

What to Bring

Swimsuit, towel, reef-safe sunscreen, cash for entry and tips. Waterproof bag for phone and ship card.

Doctor's Cave FAQ

Is Doctor's Cave Beach good for cruise passengers?›

Yes — it is one of the best beach options for Montego Bay cruise passengers. Clear water, facilities and a short drive from the port make it ideal for relaxed port days.

Should I book a beach day or an adventure tour?›

Choose Doctor's Cave if you want maximum relaxation and schedule flexibility. Choose an adventure tour if Dunn's River Falls or caves are your priority — and your port time allows longer drives.

Are Montego Bay shore excursions suitable for families?›

Doctor's Cave Beach is among the most family-friendly Montego Bay shore excursions — calm water, facilities and easy pacing for all ages.
