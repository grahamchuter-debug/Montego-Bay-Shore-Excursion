How to Choose the Right Tour

Short Port Days

Stick to Doctor's Cave Beach, Rose Hall or a Hip Strip walk. Avoid Dunn's River unless your ship arrives early and departs late.

First-Time Visitors

A beach day or Rose Hall plus shopping gives a gentle introduction. Save waterfall tours for a longer port call.

Active Travellers

Book Dunn's River Falls, river tubing or Green Grotto Caves on a cruise-timed Montego Bay shore excursion.

Families

Beach breaks and Rose Hall suit all ages. Confirm age limits on waterfall climbs and river tubing before booking.

Couples

Private driver tours and Doctor's Cave Beach days offer flexibility and romance without a crowded group schedule.

Want It All?

Ship Schedule

Cruise Timing Advice

Most Montego Bay port calls give passengers 5–8 hours ashore. A beach day or Rose Hall tour runs 3–4 hours including transport. Dunn's River Falls excursions typically need 5–6 hours because of the 60–90 minute drive each way to Ocho Rios.

Green Grotto Caves sit between Montego Bay and Ocho Rios — roughly 45–60 minutes from the cruise port. River tubing locations vary; confirm drive times when booking.

Book the earliest available departure when your ship arrives late or when multiple vessels are in port. Reputable Montego Bay cruise tour operators monitor all aboard times and build in a 60–90 minute safety buffer. Read our port guide for more planning tips.

Excursion FAQ

Common Questions

Should I book a beach day or an adventure tour?›

Beach days suit short port calls and relaxed travellers. Adventure tours to Dunn's River Falls or Green Grotto need longer drives — book only when your ship schedule allows a 5–6 hour excursion plus buffer time.

Do Montego Bay tours return to the ship on time?›

Licensed operators running Montego Bay cruise port tours track ship schedules. Confirm your all aboard time when booking and choose tours marketed specifically for cruise passengers.

Are Montego Bay shore excursions suitable for families?›

Beach breaks and Rose Hall work well for families. Dunn's River Falls suits older children who can handle moderate climbing. Check age policies on river tubing tours.

Can you visit Dunn's River Falls from Montego Bay cruise port?›

Yes, on a dedicated shore excursion. Expect 60–90 minutes driving each way. It is worth it on a long port day with an early tour departure — see our Dunn's River guide.

Can you hire a private driver in Montego Bay?›

Yes. Private driver tours let you customise your Jamaica cruise excursion — popular for couples and small groups. Read our private driver guide.
